Live performance captured and staged for VR, built so the sense of a room full of people — and the presence of live performers — survives the translation to a headset.

![The BoxOfficeVR platform showing show listings, venue pages and a theatre streaming interface across web and mobile](/media/work/bovrui.png)

The platform had to work for two very different audiences at once: people browsing shows on a phone, and people watching them in a headset — the catalogue, purchase flow and playback all had to hold up in both.
