Sensor-driven immersive sessions designed with clinicians and tested with real patients rather than demo audiences — pain management work where the immersive layer had to earn its place clinically, not just visually.

![Clinician's monitor showing a live anatomical tracking overlay alongside the patient's headset view](/media/work/ntechui.png)

Every session is monitored in real time against an anatomical model, so a clinician can see exactly what the patient is experiencing and adjust the program accordingly — the technology stays in service of the treatment plan, not the other way around.
