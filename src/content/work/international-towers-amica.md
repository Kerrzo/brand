A connected workplace ecosystem combining mobile access, parking, permits, loyalty, services and community experiences across a major commercial precinct.

## Scope

The work spanned building systems integration through to the resident-facing app — mobile access, parking and permits behaving as one coherent product rather than a collection of separate logins.

![A digital screen in the International Towers lobby advertising a lunch deal, part of the connected precinct experience](/media/work/amicaperks.png)

Perks, offers and precinct communications reach people through the same app they already use to get into the building — one identity, not a separate login for every service.
