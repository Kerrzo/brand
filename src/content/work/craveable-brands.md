A national digital ordering ecosystem connecting customers, restaurants, payments, loyalty, delivery partners and point-of-sale systems — built to hold up under real order volume, not demo traffic.

## Scope

Ordering, POS integration and loyalty were designed as one connected system rather than three separate vendors bolted together, which is where most restaurant tech stacks quietly fall apart.

![Red Rooster checkout screen showing Apple Pay, Google Pay and PayPal options alongside Red Royalty loyalty balance](/media/work/redspay.png)

Payment, loyalty redemption and delivery fees resolve in one checkout flow — Red Royalty points apply automatically before the customer reaches the pay screen.
