Pellzo brings sales, customer relationships, operations and intelligent agents into one connected business system — built for small teams who need enterprise-grade capability without enterprise-grade headcount.

## Why it exists

Most small businesses run on a patchwork of disconnected tools. Pellzo starts from the agents outward: what would it look like if the software actually did the work, not just tracked it.
