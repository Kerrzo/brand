<ArticleSection id="the-beginning">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label">01 / The beginning</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">The first time AI surprised me was in the 90s.</h2>
    <p class="body-copy">I studied Computer Science at Queen's University Belfast in the late 90s.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">One of the projects that stuck with me was an AI and computer vision exercise involving images of four different tanks.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The tanks appeared in different environments &mdash; snow, grass, jungle and sand &mdash; and from different orientations.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The challenge was to write software that could take another image we hadn't seen before and identify which tank was in it.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">We had to extract useful information from the image, correlate it and use that to make a classification.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">By today's standards it was primitive.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">At the time, it blew my mind.</p>

    <ArticleStatement size="xl" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      I'd written software that could look at something it hadn't seen before and tell me what it was.
    </ArticleStatement>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection dark id="thirty-years-later">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label" dark>Nearly 30 years later</SectionLabel>
    <ArticleStatement size="xl">
      The thing that surprised me wasn't that AI could understand software. It was that AI could help build it.
    </ArticleStatement>
    <p class="body-copy section-spacing-top-lg">When the ChatGPT moment happened, I became intensely interested in where AI was heading.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">AI-assisted development was one of the areas I explored early.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Initially, I wasn't convinced.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The tools were interesting, but the workflow was clunky. I could see the potential, but I didn't yet trust it for serious production work.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Then I got an opportunity to test that assumption properly.</p>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection id="strategy-connect">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label">02 / Strategy Connect</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">There was a platform we'd wanted to rebuild for years.</h2>
    <p class="body-copy">Years earlier, I'd built a platform for Strategy Connect.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">It wasn't a prototype or a short-lived application. It became a real production system and continued evolving over many years.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Eventually the question became inevitable: should we rebuild it?</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The problem wasn't recognising that the platform could benefit from a rebuild. The problem was economics.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">A conventional rebuild represented months of development work and significant cost.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">There was always something more immediately valuable to spend that time and money on.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">So the existing platform kept doing its job.</p>

    <ArticleStatement size="lg" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      The rebuild made sense. The economics didn't.
    </ArticleStatement>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection dark tight id="the-experiment">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label" dark>03 / The experiment</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">Then Ed asked a different question.</h2>
    <p class="body-copy">Ed, the owner of Strategy Connect, knew I'd been experimenting with AI-assisted development.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Instead of asking what a traditional rebuild would cost, he was willing to try something different.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Could we rebuild the platform using AI-assisted development?</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Neither of us knew exactly how it would go.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The tools were moving quickly.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The workflows were still emerging.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">It might work. It might not.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">But the economics of the experiment were completely different from the economics of a conventional rebuild.</p>

    <ArticleStatement size="xl" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      So we tried it.
    </ArticleStatement>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection id="seven-years-to-days">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label">04 / The rebuild</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">Then something happened that changed how I thought about software development.</h2>
    <p class="body-copy">We rebuilt the platform together.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Work that I would previously have expected to take months &mdash; potentially around six months of conventional development &mdash; happened in days.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">That distinction matters.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The original platform had accumulated years of development and product knowledge.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The new platform wasn't magically recreating seven years of work line-for-line in a few days.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">What changed was the cost and speed of turning our existing understanding of the product into working software.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">That was the moment it clicked for me.</p>

    <div class="compare-grid section-spacing-top-lg">
      <div class="metric metric--feature">
        <div class="metric__figure">~7 years</div>
        <div class="metric__label">Building + evolving the original platform</div>
      </div>
      <div class="metric metric--feature">
        <div class="metric__figure">Days</div>
        <div class="metric__label">To build the new foundation</div>
      </div>
    </div>

    <ArticleStatement size="lg" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      What I would once have estimated in months had become days.
    </ArticleStatement>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection dark id="the-boundary-moved">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label" dark>05 / What happened next</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">Then something even more interesting happened.</h2>
    <p class="body-copy">The speed of the rebuild wasn't the only thing that changed my thinking.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Ed continued developing the platform himself.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">He isn't a software developer.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Historically, someone in his position would describe a change, hand it to a developer, wait for it to be implemented, review it and repeat.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">AI-assisted development changed that relationship.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">The person who understood the business and the product could now participate directly in building the software.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">That doesn't mean software engineering disappeared.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">It means the boundary moved.</p>

    <ArticleStatement size="lg" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      First AI helped me build his software faster. Then AI helped him build his own software.
    </ArticleStatement>

    <ArticleStatement size="lg" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      That was much more interesting than code generation.
    </ArticleStatement>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection id="what-i-learned">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label">What I learned</SectionLabel>
    <h2 class="h-2">What I learned</h2>
    <ArticleLearnings items={[
      { title: "AI didn't just make development faster." },
      { title: "It changed the economics of what was worth building." },
      { title: "And it changed who could participate in building it." },
    ]} />

    <p class="body-copy section-spacing-top-lg">For most of my career, producing software was expensive.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Ideas had to justify teams, timelines and development budgets before they could become real.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">AI-assisted development changes that equation.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Code hasn't become irrelevant. Architecture hasn't become irrelevant. Engineering hasn't become irrelevant.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">But producing code is becoming dramatically cheaper.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">That shifts more value toward understanding the problem, deciding what should exist, designing the system and judging whether what has been produced is actually good.</p>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>

<ArticleSection dark tight id="final-conclusion">
  <ArticleTwoCol>
    <SectionLabel slot="label" dark>Nearly 30 years apart</SectionLabel>
    <p class="body-copy">In the late 90s, I was amazed that I could write software capable of looking at an image it hadn't seen before and deciding which tank was in it.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">Nearly thirty years later, AI surprised me again.</p>
    <p class="body-copy">But this time the software wasn't just recognising something.</p>

    <ArticleStatement size="xl" class="section-spacing-top-lg">
      It was helping write the software.
    </ArticleStatement>

    <p class="final-statement-copy" style="margin-inline:0;text-align:left;color:var(--color-accent);font-size:clamp(19px, 1.9vw, 26px);font-weight:500;letter-spacing:-.02em;">
      The tools have changed.<br />The job hasn't.
    </p>
  </ArticleTwoCol>
</ArticleSection>
