# Homepage image generation brief

Instructions for generating still images for **paulkerr.com.au** homepage placeholders.

Site context: personal brand / CTO portfolio for Paul Kerr — AI product architecture, shipped enterprise platforms, and hands-on experiments. Visual tone is editorial and product-led: warm paper background `#F3F2F0`, near-black ink `#0E0E0D`, accent `#E14A22`. Prefer real product UI, tangible craft, and grounded photography over abstract “AI glow” graphics, purple gradients, or stock hustle imagery.

**Already filled (do not regenerate):** Kerrzo project video, Strategy Connect cluster map, enterprise work photos, BoxOfficeVR / Neurotechnology heroes.

**Output:** PNG or WebP, sRGB, no baked-in UI chrome (no fake browser frames unless noted). Leave modest safe margin from edges so crops/rounded corners do not clip important content. Suggested filenames match intended `public/media/...` paths.

## Checklist for the generating AI

1. Match **exact aspect ratios** above; export at the listed pixel sizes (or larger at the same ratio).
2. Keep subjects **centred with safe margins** for rounded corners (`~12–16px` radius on site).
3. Avoid purple-neon “AI” tropes, fake logos of real brands, and unreadable micro-UI.
4. Kerrzo / Brilliancy UIs should look like **shippable product**, not marketing mockups full of lorem badges.
5. Game / lab imagery should feel like **work product from experiments**, cohesive enough to sit on one page together.
6. Deliver files named as above so they can drop into `public/media/` without renaming gymnastics.
